Developing

Plate 01 The premise

Everything in here is already gone.

Rooms with a demolition date. Shorelines under erosion review. Objects leaving public sight into private sale. Interiors that will be renovated past recognition before the year turns.

We arrive in the last hour of usable light and make one honest record. Nothing staged, nothing moved, nothing improved.

Afterwards, the place gets described from memory.
This is the part that isn’t.

Plate 02 The instrument

One body. One lens.
No second visit.

  1. i

    50mm ƒ/1.4

    Chosen because it argues least. What it sees is close to what a person standing in the room would have seen, which is the whole point of the record.

  2. ii

    Mechanical shutter

    No battery, no firmware, nothing that can decline to work inside a building with the power already cut. It has never once failed to open.

  3. iii

    Brass through black

    Fifteen years of thumbs have worn the paint back to metal at every edge a hand finds. We don’t refinish it. The wear is a record of its own.

Plate 05 Method

The last-light protocol.

  1. 01

    Arrive with ninety minutes of daylight remaining.

  2. 02

    Walk the whole floor before the first frame is made.

  3. 03

    One plate per aspect. No bracketing. No second chances.

  4. 04

    Move nothing, improve nothing, remove no one.

  5. 05

    Record the minute the light failed.

  6. 06

    Leave before dark. The building is owed that much.

Plate 06 Access

The archive is open
by request.

Access is granted to researchers, heritage bodies, and owners of the places recorded. Commissions are accepted where a site has a confirmed end date — the sooner before it, the better the record.

Requests are answered in the order the sites are due to disappear.
