Haar Overshirt

$245

A shirt with a jacket’s ambitions. Cut from 10oz waxed cotton that starts stiff and gives in after a fortnight, with a boxy body sized to close over a cable knit without pulling. Named for the cold sea fog that rolls in off the North Sea and ruins an otherwise reasonable morning.

Size

In stock — dispatched within two working days

Details
  • 10oz waxed cotton, undyed horn buttons
  • Boxy body, dropped shoulder, single chest pocket
  • Two-piece collar that stands when turned up
  • Cut and finished in Portugal, 180 made per run
Cloth & care

Woven at a family mill in Lancashire and waxed in-house. Do not machine wash — brush clean, and re-wax once a year with any paraffin dressing. The finish will lighten at the elbows and collar; this is the point of it.

Size & fit

Cut generously. Our fit model is 183cm and wears a M. If you are between sizes and intend to layer knitwear underneath, stay with your usual size — the body is already cut to allow for it.

Shipping & returns

Complimentary shipping over $150. Returns accepted within 30 days, unworn, with the wax finish intact. We pay return postage on anything that doesn’t fit.
