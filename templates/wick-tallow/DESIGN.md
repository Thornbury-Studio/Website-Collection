# WICK — Tallow Works

A single-game site. Not a studio brochure, not an arcade, not a card game.

## DNA (extracted, not copied)

Visited, then rebuilt as a different game:

| Source | What we took | What we refused |
|---|---|---|
| [SUPERHOT](https://superhotgame.com/) | The site *performs* the mechanic. One sentence is the brand. Extreme reduction. | Their red world, scanlines, low-poly costume, time-moves-when-you-move. |
| [Playdead](https://playdead.com/) | Cinema stills do the talking. Chrome almost disappears. Empty of people. | Their greyscale, spider/child silhouettes, gallery-of-games grid. |
| [Expedition 33](https://www.expedition33.com/) | A world-rule as a line you can say out loud. Wishlist as the ask. | Belle Époque painting, numbered death, a cast of faces. |

v4 framed work on paper. v5 was film on sand. v6 was a wallpaper of other sites. This fills the viewport with one factory, and the pointer is the match.

## The game

**WICK.** You are in Tallow Works, a shuttered tropical match factory. You have one lit match. Moving fast spends the flame. Standing still lets you see. Fifty matches in the box you found; each death costs one.

The line: **Don't run.**

## The site's one authored moment

The photographs are dark. A match-cone follows the pointer (touch, on a phone). Velocity shrinks the cone — rush and the room goes out. Type and controls stay fully lit. Reduced motion parks a wide still cone. Keyboard users never need the cone to read.

That is Superhot's "the page is the rule," inverted: motion costs you.

Type: **Sofia Sans Extra Condensed** (the sleeve), **Figtree** (voice), **Azeret Mono** (notices, the count). Not Inter, not Fraunces, not Unbounded, not Big Shoulders.

## Pages

1. `index.html` — the dip hall. The line. The count. Wishlist.
2. `house.html` — four floors as full-bleed chapters, not a card grid.
3. `manual.html` — the rule as a factory notice.
4. `box.html` — the sleeve, platforms, the list.

## What this is not

- Not NIGHTSHIFT (a venue).
- Not FATHOM (paper cards, Fraunces, a trawl).
- Not FRACTURE (a fight card, snapping debris).
- No faces in the photographs (AI-face cap). No purple, no particles, no bento.
