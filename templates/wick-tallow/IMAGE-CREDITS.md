# Image credits — wick-tallow (WICK)

Every still on this site was generated for it. Nothing is licensed stock.

Gemini prepaid credits were depleted (`RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED` on
`nano-banana-pro-preview`, `gemini-3.1-flash-image`, and
`gemini-2.5-flash-image`) so the set was generated in Cursor image-gen with
one STYLE held across the batch, then restaged from the approved dip-hall
master. Packing, vault and yard were re-rolled once to kill gibberish
lettering. No video.

## STYLE (verbatim in every prompt)

Night interior (or night exterior for the yard) of a shuttered tropical match factory in maritime Southeast Asia, photographed as if on 35mm tungsten film. The only light is a single struck match. Palette strictly soot black, wax-cream wood, dull iron, and the orange of the flame. NO people faces, NO text, NO numbers, NO logos, NO labels.
