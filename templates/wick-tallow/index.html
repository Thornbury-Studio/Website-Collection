The only rule

Motion costs light.

Tallow Works dipped its last batch in March 1974 and locked the doors with the racks still wet. You are inside with whatever is left in the box. There is no torch. There is no second floor of emergency lighting. If the match dies, you strike another, until the box is empty.

Found, packing floor

The wood is still waiting to be heads.

They left the splints in the racks. Phosphorus is in the vault. Wax is in the troughs. The strike strip on the wall still takes a head if you drag it slow. Drag it fast and it shrugs you off.
