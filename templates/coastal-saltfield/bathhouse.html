Across the garden

The bathhouse

Steam, cold water,
and no clock on the wall.

A low building of lime plaster and pale stone on the far side of the kitchen garden. A hot room that smells of cedar, a plunge the temperature of the sea in March, two deep tubs, and a bench against the south wall for afterwards. Phones stay in the basket by the door.

Inside

Four rooms,
one temperature at a time.

The hot room first, as long as you can. The plunge second, quicker than you'd like. The tubs third, as long as you want. The bench last, wrapped in wool, watching the garden do nothing in particular.

Towels, robes and salt scrub are in the anteroom; nothing to carry over but yourself.

Hours

Mornings shared, evenings booked

The evening hour is yours alone — ask at the kitchen for the slate.
