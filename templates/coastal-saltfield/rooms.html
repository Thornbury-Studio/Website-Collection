Rooms & rates

Six rooms, no two alike.

Each room is named for what it looks at or where it came from, priced by season, and held for you at two nights or more. Rates below are each room's quiet-months price; the exact figure for your dates is worked out to the night when you book.

Rates

Three seasons, one rule

Every night is priced in the season it falls in — a stay across a boundary is priced night by night.
