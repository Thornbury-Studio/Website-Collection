Enquire · 2026

Ask about a departure.

Eleven of this year's thirty-one departures still have places. Tell us which route and roughly when, and someone who has walked it will write back within two working days.

What happens next

A reply from a person, not a brochure.

We will tell you honestly whether the departure suits you: the longest day, the ascent, the nights under canvas if there are any. If it doesn't, we will say which one does. Places are held for seven days once we have agreed one, and nothing is paid until then.

If you would rather write directly: field@longlight.no. We don't do phones; the guides are usually somewhere without one.
