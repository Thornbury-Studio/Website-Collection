The range

Pick a can.
The site follows.

Those four dots in the header are the range. Choose one and everything on every page — panels, buttons, even the ink films — re-brews itself in that flavour. The cans wear each other's colours as a band, because no can is an island.

The party trick

One of these changes colour in the glass

Mood Ring is brewed on butterfly pea flower — a natural pH indicator. Pour it, squeeze the lime, and it flips from indigo to violet to hot pink while you watch. Chemistry, not colouring.
