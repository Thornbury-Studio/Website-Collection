Studio

One room. Three people. A lot of daylight.

Thornbury Digital is a design studio in Singapore. We design and build websites for companies that need to look like they mean it, and we do it in one room with the sun coming through a screen of ventilation blocks, which is why this site is lit the way it is.

Right now, in the room

It is — and the sun is — above the horizon, bearing —: daylight. That is the light on this page.

The screen

Ventilation blocks · The tropical-modernist answer to sun and rain

Cast concrete with the middle cut out, stacked into a wall that breathes. They shade a room without sealing it, and as the sun moves the pattern moves across the floor. Every page here is lit through one, by the real sun, at the real time.

How we work

Three things we do every time.

01

We measure before we touch anything

What your site loads, what it says, and what it fails to say to a machine reading it aloud. Speed, structure and search, found before a single opinion is offered.

02

Drawn for the words you actually have

Composition, type and colour chosen against your real content, not a layout you fill in afterwards and hope fits. If the words do not exist yet, we say so, and we scope writing them.

03

You leave with the keys

Repo, domain, mailbox, analytics. Nothing stays on our side after handover, including the ability to leave us. A site is the only member of staff that never sleeps; it should belong to you.
