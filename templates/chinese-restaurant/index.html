    點菜之道 How to order

    1. 01

      One dish per person

      The rough rule for a shared table. Four people, four dishes — then a rice on top.

    2. 02

      Spread the cooking

      Something steamed, something fried, something braised. Not four of the same thing.

    3. 03

      Always a green

      It resets the palate between the heavy dishes. The table tells you if you've skipped it.

    4. 04

      Rice last

      It arrives with the mains, not before. It's there to carry sauce, not to fill you up first.
