GIG Cafe's website, menu PDFs, social accounts, or any listing site. Their menu
images were read once for *factual* extraction (dish names and prices) and no
pixel of them is reused.

## Grading

Every frame — stock included — went through one identical pass
(`grade.py`, scratchpad): +1.2% red / −0.8% blue warm shift, a 14% smoothstep
S-curve, colour 0.97, brightness 1.015, then responsive WebP export at q82.
No per-image tweaking.

## Original work

`img/favicon.svg` and the inline five-petal bloom mark are original drawings

decoration are deliberately not used or imitated; the bloom mark is a new
