FAQ

Straight answers.

How fast is the first live loop?

Thirty days for a named outcome — usually demand sensing into replenishment. Six to eight weeks to have the first production workflows owned by your operators.

Do you replace the ERP?

No. ERP stays in the ecosystem. It stops being the control tower. RADIX is the operating layer that senses, decides, and acts on live data.

What is Verid?

A human identity bank. Face, voice, and persona are bound, monitored, and licensed. It is how RADIX addresses the damage AI can do to people, not only to P&L.

Where are you based?

450 Park Avenue, Floor 32, New York. Work is done with client operators, on their floor and in their systems.
