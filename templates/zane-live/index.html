01 / Topics

What we actually talk about

Six things come up every week. None of them need a supplement stack to get started.

  • 01

    Getting fit from zero

    Where to actually begin when you have never touched a barbell — and the loud advice worth ignoring for your first three months.

  • 02

    Gym routines

    Programmes that survive a real week — work, school, NS, a body that is already tired. What to cut when you only have forty minutes.

  • 03

    Nutrition

    Eating for training on hawker food and a normal budget. Protein you can get without walking down the powder aisle.

  • 04

    Physical check-ins

    Weight, lifts, sleep, how the week actually went. Said out loud on air, which makes it much harder to quietly fudge.

  • 05

    Video review

    Gym clips, nutrition takes and fail videos, watched together — what is fine, what is nonsense, and what will actually hurt you.

  • 06

    Myths & mistakes

    The advice that keeps circulating and refuses to die. Usually because somebody at the end of it is selling something.

02 / Rundown

How the hour runs

The same five cues every week, so you know what you are joining and when. Times are Singapore time.

  1. Cue 01 19:0008 min

    Warm-up topic

    Whatever came up during the week — a question from the comments, a video doing the rounds, something that happened in the gym.

    On now
  2. Cue 02 19:0812 min

    The check-up

    Physical check-in and progress talk. Numbers, body weight, what moved this week and what stubbornly did not.

    On now
  3. Cue 03 19:2015 min

    Video review

    Gym, nutrition and fail clips watched on air, frame by frame, with a plain explanation of what is actually going on.

    On now
  4. Cue 04 19:3515 min

    The breakdown

    One routine or one day of eating, taken apart properly — sets, reasons, and what would change if your week looked different.

    On now
  5. Cue 05 19:5010 min

    Live chat & Q&A

    Straight questions from the chat, answered straight. When the honest answer is “it depends”, we say what it depends on.

    On now

Total 60 minutes Cues shift a little when the chat is good. That is the point of doing it live.

03 / Why watch

Why it is worth an hour

  • A

    Nothing is being sold to you

    No supplement code, no coaching funnel, no twelve-week transformation package waiting at the end. The show is the whole thing.

  • B

    Built for a normal week

    Most people train around a job, school or NS — not around a meal-prep schedule and a free afternoon. That is who this is for.

  • C

    Singapore, specifically

    Hawker centre food, gyms you can actually get to, the humidity, what things cost here. Advice that survives contact with where you live.

  • D

    Live means accountable

    Progress gets reported on air, on a fixed schedule. The bad weeks get said out loud too, which is the part most fitness content skips.

  • E

    Real examples, not theory

    Every claim gets checked against a clip, a number, or somebody's actual training week — including mine.
