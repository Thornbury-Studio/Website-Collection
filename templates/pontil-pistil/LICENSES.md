## Libraries

None at runtime. The page uses no third-party script, no CDN and no framework: `js/relief.js` is a raw WebGL2 program and `js/main.js` is plain DOM code, both written for this site.

## Build-time only (nothing of it ships)

- sharp (Apache-2.0), for resizing and encoding images.
- @huggingface/transformers (Apache-2.0), to run the depth model.
- Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0). The larger V2 checkpoints are CC-BY-NC-4.0 and were not used.
- Playwright (Apache-2.0), to render the social card.
