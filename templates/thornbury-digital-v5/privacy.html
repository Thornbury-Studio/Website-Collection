06 — Privacy

Privacy

What this site collects, which is almost nothing. Updated 7 September 2026.

I — What we collect

Nothing, until you write to us.

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels. When you send the brief or email us, we receive your name, your email address, your company if you give it, and what you wrote.

II — Why, and for how long

Why
To read your message and reply to it. Nothing else: no marketing, no list, not sold, and not shared beyond the services that carry it.
How long
Your message exists as one email in our inbox and stays there until we delete it. There is no other copy.
Who carries it
Resend delivers the email. Vercel hosts the site and keeps ordinary server logs under its own policy. The typeface comes from Google Fonts and one animation library from jsDelivr; those servers see your IP address when a page loads, as any server you fetch a file from does.
Where it sits in law
We are a Singapore studio, and the Personal Data Protection Act governs anything you send us.

III — Your rights

See it, correct it, or have it deleted.

Write to contact@thornburystudio.com. A person reads it and replies.

If what this site collects ever changes, this page changes with it. The date at the top is the date the site last behaved differently.
