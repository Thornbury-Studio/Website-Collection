01 · The idea

A machine that stands still is falling.

Every motorcycle is an argument with gravity that only holds while you keep moving. We think that is the single most honest fact in engineering — so we named the company after the physics, and we named the machines after what riding actually does: the apex, the camber of a road, the rake of a fork, the trail it casts, the slip of a tyre.

GYRE machines wear their engineering on the outside. One unbroken halo of light. One exposed structural spine. One volt line tracing every body. Nothing decorative that isn't also true.

03 · Design DNA
The halo

One unbroken circle of light on every machine. Never split, never abbreviated — the marque's signature, readable at three hundred metres.

The spine

A cast structural member in raw ash-grey alloy, run from steering head to tail and left in plain sight. The frame is the bodywork.

The open wheel

A single-sided swingarm on every machine, so the rear wheel reads as a complete disc — motion, shown whole.
