# COL NOIR — asset credits

All video is free-license 4K footage from Pexels (Pexels License: free for
commercial use, no attribution required — credited here anyway). Downloaded
2026-08-11 at 3840×2160 / 4096×2160, re-encoded for the web (H.264 1440p
faststart MP4; the hero also ships a 3840×2160 VP9 WebM tier). The base-village
clip was shot in a flat D-log profile and was graded in the encode
(contrast/saturation/gamma). The three OG stills were graded with the Adobe
Photoshop/Lightroom API (contrast +12, highlights −14, vibrance +14, slight
warm shift) for one crisp alpine look.
