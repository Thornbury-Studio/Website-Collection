# LONG TABLE — image provenance

LONG TABLE is a **fictional restaurant** built as a portfolio template. The name, address,
prices, hours and the entire seven-day rotation are invented. No real restaurant's identity
is used.

## Photography — licensed Adobe Stock (free tier)

All 8 photographs were searched, licensed and downloaded on **8 August 2026** through the
Adobe Stock connector, free-tier assets only (license state `just_purchased`). Every asset was
checked to be `isGenTech: false` — nothing here is AI-generated, and nothing is scraped.

The grade is deliberately the lightest in the collection (Pillow: +3% brightness, +4%
contrast, +2% saturation) — a buffet sells on freshness, so the images stay close to neutral.
Stations are cropped to 900 × 640; the hero is 1920 × 1150.

## What is real and what is not

There is deliberately no ordering flow — a buffet site's job is information. What *is* live:
the seven-day rotation is data-driven and defaults to the actual current weekday, and the
"Open now / Opens at" pill in the header is computed from the visitor's clock against the
posted hours, updating every minute. The phone number is a 555 number.
