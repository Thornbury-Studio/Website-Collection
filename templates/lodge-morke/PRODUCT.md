# PRODUCT.md — MØRKE

The product truth for this template. Everything below is invented for the
showcase, except the astronomy, which is real and checked (`tools/check-season.mjs`).

## The business

- **Name:** MØRKE (written Mørke in running text; *mørke* is Norwegian for "dark").
  Legal line: Mørke Lodge AS.
- **What:** a six-room expedition lodge on a frozen shore in Svalbard, a former
  1930s trapper's station rebuilt in black-stained larch.
- **Where:** 78°26′ N 15°57′ E. 64 km and about three hours by snowmobile from the
  airport at Longyearbyen, which is about three hours' flight from Oslo.
- **When:** only during the polar night, 26 October 2026 to 16 February 2027. It opens
  the first morning the sun doesn't rise and closes the day it comes back.
- **Who:** twelve guests at most, with one guide to every four on every trip out. Guides
  carry the polar-bear protection Svalbard law requires outside settlements.
- **Rooms:** four doubles and two twins, each with a north-facing window.
- **Food:** dinner at seven at one long table (Arctic char, reindeer, bread from the oven).
- **Sauna:** wood-fired, 80 °C, at the water's edge, with an ice hole beside it.
- **Stays:** 4, 5 or 7 nights, arriving on Mondays and Thursdays.
- **Price:** NOK 11,400 per person per night, sharing. It includes the snowmobile
  transfer both ways, guiding, all meals, the sauna, suits and boots. Flights to
  Longyearbyen are not included.
- **Enquiries:** booking@morkelodge.no. The site's form writes the email; there is no
  backend (AGENT.md).

## Facts the page states (real, computed for 78.43° N)

- **Last sunrise:** 25 October. The sun is then below the horizon for 113 days.
- **Back:** 16 February.
- **12 November to 30 January:** the sun stays more than 6° below the horizon all day,
  so there is no civil twilight even at noon.
- **Noon on 21 December:** 11.9° below the horizon.
- **The daytime aurora:** Svalbard sits under the dayside auroral oval, so aurora can
  appear at any hour of the polar night.

## Audience and voice

- **Audience:** people who already know what an aurora looks like on a phone screen and want
  the real season: the dark itself, not a northern-lights tour.
- **Voice:** plain, specific, a little dry. Numbers instead of adjectives. One host's sentence
  per chapter ("We've had them over breakfast.").
- **Banned:** "unforgettable", "once-in-a-lifetime", "magical", "Empower/Unlock/Transform",
  any promise that the aurora will appear.
