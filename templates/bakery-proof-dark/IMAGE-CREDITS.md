All media comes from **Pexels**, downloaded on 2026-11-04 without an account, under the **Pexels License** (https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, no attribution required; not to be sold unaltered, not to imply endorsement, identifiable people not shown in a bad light, not used as a trademark. Each clip's resolution was checked against its public file (3840x2160 or 4096x2160); the masters are never committed. The shortlist, including every rejected item, was reviewed and approved by the boss before any master was downloaded.
