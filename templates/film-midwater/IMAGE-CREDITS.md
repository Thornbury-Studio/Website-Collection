# MIDWATER — asset credits

All video is free-license 4K footage from Pexels (Pexels License: free for
commercial use, no attribution required — credited anyway, in the site's own
credit roll and here). Downloaded 2026-08-11 at 3840×2160, re-encoded for the
web (H.264 1440p/1080p faststart MP4 + VP9 4K WebM for the hero). Stills are
frames extracted from the same clips, batch-graded into one film look with
Adobe Photoshop/Lightroom API adjustments (cool temperature, −18 saturation,
deepened darks, recovered highlights; whale frames got a second blue-channel
pass).
