Platform

Four systems, one operating picture

01 · VISIBILITY

Every shipment, one picture

Carrier EDI, terminal feeds and AIS positions resolve into a single tracked object per shipment — not three portals and a spreadsheet. Position confidence is displayed, never assumed; a stale ping is marked stale.

02 · PREDICTION

Delay exposure, priced in days

Congestion, dwell, weather and schedule reliability roll into a 14-day forecast per lane, with confidence attached. You see which POs slip, which buffers absorb it, and the date the first line stops — while there is still time to act.

03 · REROUTING

Options before commitments

When a lane degrades, Northline drafts the alternatives — port swap, carrier swap, modal split — each carried to landed cost, CO₂ and the customer’s due date. Your team approves a decision, not a scramble.

04 · EXCEPTIONS

Triage that respects your morning

Every alert carries its consequence: shipments exposed, first inventory impact, cost of doing nothing. Noise is suppressed at the source — a vessel four hours late into a two-week buffer is not an exception, and Northline knows it.
