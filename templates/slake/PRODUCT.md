# Slake — product truth

## Who this is for
People who already pay Fever-Tree / Fentimans / Fly By Jing money for a
drink with an actual point of view, and who think most "spicy" ginger
beer is a sugar drink with a ginger smell. They drink it neat over ice,
or with tequila/mezcal, and they'd rather cough on the first sip than
be bored.

## What it does / sells
One product: a 250 ml bottle of small-batch ginger soda made from 38 g of
pressed fresh ginger, charred ginger skins steeped for the smoke, cane
sugar, lime, carbonated spring water. Sold by the case of twelve. The
site's one job is to make a visitor feel the burn-then-relief before
tasting it, and to sell them a case.

## Voice & tone
Plain, dry, a little cocky, exact about numbers. Says what's in the
bottle and what it does to you. Never "crafted", "artisanal", "journey",
"elevate".

## Constraints that are actually load-bearing right now
1. The heat → cold transition must be *structural*: the page physically
   changes register as you scroll (one variable, `--heat`), not two
   moodboards side by side.
2. Real people, real footage: licensed Adobe Stock only, no AI-generated
   faces, no rendered product shot. The pour is the product.

## What makes this one memorable
You scroll and the page cools down. The coals become the pour, the black
becomes frost, and the headline finishes its sentence: "It burns." →
"Then it doesn't." That's the drink.
