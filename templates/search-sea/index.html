      Why SEA

      Search used to be one habit. Now you look up, ask, and browse — three instincts, one keyboard. SEA is the adapter that keeps them in a single box.

      Listen once

      Point the browser’s search here. You stop choosing a tab; the words choose the destination.

      Questions are different

      ? means you want an answer → ChatGPT. “What is…” means a page → Wikipedia. Anything else → Google.

      Thin on purpose

      No app, no login. A compiled Nginx map. The meaning stays in how you type.
