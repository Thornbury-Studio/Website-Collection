      Why SEA exists

      Search used to mean one engine. Now it means three habits — look up, ask, and browse — jammed into the same keyboard. SEA is the adapter that keeps those habits in one box.

      One listening post

      You should not have to remember which site answers which kind of thought. Point the browser’s search at SEA. It hears the shape of the words and sends them where they belong.

      A question is not a keyword

      End with a ? and you want an answer, not ten blue links. SEA routes that to ChatGPT. Ask what something is, and Wikipedia gets the track. Everything else falls to Google.

      No app to babysit

      There is no dashboard and no login wall. SEA is a thin Nginx proxy — a compiled map of rules. The meaning stays in how you type; the machinery stays out of the way.

      When you are ready to run it on your own server, the config lives with Dense Analysis — open source, no ceremony.
