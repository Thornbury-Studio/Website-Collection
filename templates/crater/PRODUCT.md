# CRATER — product truth

This file plus this template's `DESIGN.md` (which inherits
`../../../DESIGN-SYSTEM/DESIGN.md`) is what any design or copy pass reads
first. Every number below is also in `js/site.js` `CATALOGUE`; the page
renders prices and dates from there, never from typed copy.

## Who this is for
Someone who brews coffee by hand at home, or is about to start, and has been
told "fresh" by every bag they've bought. They can't taste the difference
between two descriptions; they *can* see a bed of grounds rise. Second
reader: a gift buyer who wants one good bag, not a menu.

## The one claim
Fresh coffee blooms: hot water drives the roasting gas out of fresh grounds
and the bed rises, splits and settles, violently for about ten seconds and
mostly over by forty-five. Stale or pre-ground coffee barely moves. The site
proves this with real footage at real speed instead of saying "fresh".

## Real-world facts the copy relies on
- Volcán de Fuego (3,763 m) is continuously active and sends up small ash
  plumes many times a day; it faces the Acatenango Valley across a ravine.
- Soil weathered from volcanic ash is porous, mineral-rich and holds water;
  Guatemala's dry season runs roughly November to April.
- Carbon dioxide from roasting leaves whole beans slowly and ground coffee
  within hours; while it streams out it keeps water from wetting the grounds,
  which is why brewers wait out the bloom.

## Voice & tone
Sensory and specific, short sentences, understated. Let the physical fact do
the persuading. Banned: "ethically sourced", "hand-picked", "artisan",
"journey" as a noun, and any adjective a buyer couldn't check in their own cup.

## Not claimed
No farm, cooperative or producer is named or implied. No certifications, no
awards, no reviews, no founder story, no team photographs.
