La Maison

Savoir-attendre.

Savoir-faire is knowing how to make. Savoir-attendre is knowing how to hold the receipt and look at nothing in particular. The Maison practises only the second.

  1. Minute 0

    The order is placed. The Maison is founded at the counter and has no other premises.

  2. Minute 2

    A receipt is issued, bearing a number. The number is adopted as the house’s coat of arms.

  3. Minute 4

    The first estimate, “about ten minutes”, is announced. It becomes the standing figure and has not been revised.

  4. Minute 9

    A table is chosen. The Maison does not otherwise decorate.

  5. Minute 14

    The Seated Collection is unveiled: one piece, in grey.

  6. Today

    Independent, unlisted, and owned entirely by whoever holds the receipt.
