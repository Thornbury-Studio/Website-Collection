Honey

Seven honeys, because the hedge gave us seven different fortnights.

Most honey on a shelf is blended to taste the same every year. Ours cannot be: we pull the supers as each flow ends and extract them separately, so a jar of lime is lime and nothing else. It also means we sell out, in an order that changes annually.

The year’s account

What the hedge gave, and what we kept

A colony gathers a great deal more than it gives up. Most of the year’s nectar is eaten by the bees that fetched it, or by the brood they were feeding at the time. What reaches a jar is the remainder, after we have left them enough to winter on.
