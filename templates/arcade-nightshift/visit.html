The practical page

Visit

Everything you need before you arrive: when we’re open, what it costs, where we are, and the small number of rules that keep ninety-two machines and one kitchen running smoothly.

Night Pass

The Night Pass is a stored-value card: load it once, tap it at any machine. Credits never expire, passes are shareable within your group, and the balance reads out at every cabinet.

Hours

Open every night of the year, including the ones you’d expect us to close.

Rooms

Four private rooms for crews, birthdays and grudge matches — your machines, your playlist, your door. Booked by the hour with a dedicated host on call.

Getting here

AddressB1, 88 Meridian Walk — the amber N above the escalators.

Late exitAfter midnight, leave via the B1 lobby — the night concierge will point you to the ranks.

House rules

  • Queues are sacred. The machine decides nothing; the queue decides everything.
  • Spectating is free and encouraged. Coaching from the rail is at your own risk.
  • Under-16s are welcome until 21:00 with an adult who understands they will lose to them.
  • Food stays in TABLE. The machines have opinions about crumbs.
  • If a machine misbehaves, tell the floor crew — don’t percussive-maintain it.

Accessibility

The whole floor is step-free, including ROOMS and the mezzanine (lift by the VAULT entrance). Hairpin and Slipstep have transfer-assist configurations and our crew are trained and unhurried about setting them up. VOLT runs a lower-volume hour from 10:00 to 11:00 daily. Ear defenders live at the front desk, free against a pass. Every menu at TABLE is available in large print, and the whole venue is navigable without stairs, music, or small talk if that’s the night you’re having.

Questions

Do I need to book?

No — walk in, load a pass, play. Book only for ROOMS, Kingmaker team slots, or groups of ten or more. Friday after ten is busiest; the floor breathes again after midnight.

How much does a night cost?

Entry is free. Games run 8–20 credits, and the $50 load covers a serious evening for one or a decent one for two. The maths is on the pass page and the pass never expires, so an ambitious load isn’t a gamble.

What happens at 02:00?

Last races start at 01:40, the floor clears by two, and the building goes onto its night schedule — machines to standby, cases dark, cameras on. That’s the theory, anyway.

Can I hire the whole venue?

Yes — the venue takes private full-floor bookings before 18:00. If you want it after dark, that conversation happens over email and coffee.

I left something behind.

Lost property lives at the front desk for ninety days. Describe it, don’t name it — you’d be surprised how many people claim to have lost “a black umbrella”.
