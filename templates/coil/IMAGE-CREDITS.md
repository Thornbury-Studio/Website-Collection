# COIL — image, font and asset credits

Every image on the page is a real photograph. Nothing on the page was
generated by an AI image or video tool, and no generation tool was used for
this template at all (the Higgsfield ask-first checkpoint was never reached:
the tin was made by editing a licensed photograph).

Licences were checked per asset on 23 Sep 2026, not assumed from the site:

- **Unsplash** photos were checked through `unsplash.com/napi/photos/<id>`
  from an unsplash.com tab: `premium: false` and `plus: false` (so the
  Unsplash Licence applies, not Unsplash+), plus the recorded location.
- **Pexels** photos were checked through the `__NEXT_DATA__` of the Pexels
  search and photo pages fetched from a pexels.com tab: `license: "Pexels"`
  and the creator's name. (Pexels *video* pages still sit behind a bot check;
  no video is used.)
- **Adobe Stock** images are from the free collection, licensed through the
  Adobe connector (`asset_license_and_download_stock`), `isGenTech: false`,
  and checked for camera EXIF (both are real photographs).

The full-resolution originals live in `tools/raw/` (gitignored). They are cut,
graded and exported by `tools/grade.py` and `tools/tin.py`.

## Rejected, and why

- **Pexels video 8255164** ("Close up of Tea Leaves Brewing in a Glass
  Kettle", ArtHouse Studio). Cut into a tracked, looped 1920 px band by a
  waterline tracker (the source is handheld), then dropped: soft focus,
  muddy olive colour and a different, broken leaf. It was the only real
  steeping clip in any free library, and it was weaker than the stills.
  No real footage of a rolled oolong opening exists on Unsplash, Pexels,
  Pixabay, Adobe Stock (free), Mixkit or Coverr as of 23 Sep 2026.
- **Pexels 6713017** ("Press machine with rolled tea", Quang Nguyen Vinh,
  photographed in Vietnam). A real cross-section of leaf rolled in cloth,
  literally a coil. Used in the first build, then removed after the design
  critique: its saturated factory colour broke the single photographic voice.
- **Pexels 35110204** (a used teabag, Marek Ruczaj) and **Pexels 35797059**
  (CTC granules on a spoon, Marek Ruczaj): the first did not read as dust;
  the second is itself a rolled pellet and would blur the comparison.
- **Unsplash `4orQSKpTKPI`**: listed under "teabag", actually loose Earl Grey.
- **Taiwanese tea-garden photographs**: every one found was taken in Chiayi
  (Alishan, Zhuqi), New Taipei, or an unrecorded place. None in Nantou, so
  none is used as the garden.
- **Pexels 2171548**, a circular tea garden (Vietnam): would imply the place.
