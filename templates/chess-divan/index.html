      The room

      Come and play the next one.

      Divan is a first-floor room above a tea merchant in Covent Garden. Fourteen tables, a kettle that never goes cold, and one house rule: touch a piece, move it.

      The name is borrowed from the chess-and-coffee houses of 1850s London that called themselves divans. We kept the coffee, added the tea, and left out the clocks unless you ask for one.

      Every Thursday at 19:30 we put a famous game on the big board and walk through it the way you just did: a move at a time, with someone arguing about the tenth.
