What is Zari?

A word that means gold thread.

Food is all about its story — the origin, the spices, the texture and the taste of every dish. It is the cornerstone of culture, of ceremony, of nights worth remembering.

ZARI weaves those stories into an evening: the finest Indian cuisine, served with careful attention to every detail of food, service and ambience.
