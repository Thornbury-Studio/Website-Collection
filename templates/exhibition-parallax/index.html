PARALLAX

Parallax · The Catalogue

Every object here would fail instantly, anywhere else.

PARALLAX is an international exhibition of impossible objects — matter, architecture and organisms that could not survive physical reality for a single frame. The browser is the only room that will hold them. Admission begins with Exhibit 00, which you have just walked through: the exhibition’s first impossible object, a building made of nothing, entered from anywhere, larger inside than out.
