01 · THE WORLD

61.1° N · BASALT · CLOSED TO THE PUBLIC ROAD NETWORK

One peninsula.
Five predators.

KARN does not build a range. It builds answers — one machine per sector of the proving peninsula the company has closed, instrumented and worshipped since 1994. The salt flat got a record car. The ring got a wing on wheels. The ash dunes got a two-tonne argument. The coast road got the night itself. And the works got a prototype with its skin half off.

Nothing on this site is an adjective. Every figure — every top speed, every 0–100, every newton of downforce — is computed live from each machine's declared mass, power and drag by the same model our engineers sign. The conditions on the chart are today's. The peninsula does not do brochures.
