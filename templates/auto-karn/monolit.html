On the Weiss Flat there is nothing to hide behind — no apex to flatter you, no wall to blame. There is drag, there is power, and there is the crust waiting to read your line. So the MONOLIT is honest the way a bullet is honest: a teardrop canopy, a tail as long as patience, and every gram interrogated before it was allowed aboard.

The figures on the right are not claims. They fall out of the mass, the power and the drag area printed above them, through the same model that plans our record mornings. Change one primitive and every number below it would move. That is what makes them true.
