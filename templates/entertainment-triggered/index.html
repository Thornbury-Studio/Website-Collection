05 — Questions

The short
answers.

Do I need to book ahead?

Walk-ins are accepted but not guaranteed — slots fill up, especially at peak. Booking online is the safe option.

How young can players be?

Six is the minimum, and under-fives can't enter the rooms at all. Between 6 and 12 an adult has to be in the room, holding a ticket, playing or not. Three of the rooms need players to be 13+.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

No refunds for cancellations, no-shows or late arrivals. You can reschedule with at least 24 hours' notice; same-day changes aren't possible.

How do I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Amex and PayNow. Online payment is required at the time of booking.

What's the smallest group you'll take?

Two players. The ceiling depends on the room — six for Hide & Seek, eight for Press It!, ten for the rest.

Is there really a free bonus for playing twice?

Yes — book the same room back-to-back and you get an extra 10 minutes at no cost. It doesn't apply to Hide & Seek or Press It!.
