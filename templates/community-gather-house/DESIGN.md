# Gather House

**Thesis.** A neighbourhood house for gathering — walnut timber, plaster walls, soft evening light. Intimate workshops, long-table dinners, and private hire in Tampines. Not a ballroom, not a co-working brand: one warm room that people return to.

**Palette.** Foam `#f7f3ec` ground · plaster `#ebe4d8` panels · walnut `#3a2a1c` brand · charcoal `#1a1714` text · amber `#c4853a` accent only.

**Type.** Fraunces for the house name and headlines; Figtree for body and chrome.

**Pages.**
1. **Home** — full-bleed muted hero video; brand-first; one line; Book / Gallery CTAs; short intro; hire + events teasers.
2. **Venue hire** — Half Day / Full Day / Evening Social; amenities; two room photographs; contact CTA.
3. **Events** — four upcoming static programmes.
4. **Gallery** — ten stills + one clip; lightbox on click; staggered reveal.
5. **Contact** — name, email, date, party size, message; client validate; mailto success.

**Motion.** Hero fade-up; scroll reveals; gallery stagger. CSS + IntersectionObserver. `prefers-reduced-motion` kills transitions and video autoplay.
