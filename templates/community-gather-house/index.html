Walnut floors, plaster walls, evening light.

East of the expressway · west of the park

Gather House is a single warm room in Tampines — built for workshops that run late, dinners that stretch past dessert, and private hire that still feels like someone’s home.

We keep the capacity intimate on purpose. Soft seating along the long wall, a kitchenette that actually works, and timber that holds the sound of a room without swallowing it. Come for a morning clay circle or an evening social; leave the banquet halls to someone else.
