# Image and footage credits — Circle Take

Every frame on this site is real, filmed footage from Pexels. Nothing is
generated. Each clip was previewed before use and its page checked on
2026-11-03: the Pexels Licence (https://www.pexels.com/license/) allows
free commercial use and modification, attribution not required (given here
anyway). Its limits that matter here: identifiable people may not be shown
in a bad light, and the footage may not imply that the people in it endorse
a product. The site therefore never names the people on screen and carries
no testimonials attributed to them.

The raw downloads are not in this repository. `tools/film.sh` and
`tools/previews.sh` rebuild every file below from the originals
(`https://www.pexels.com/download/video/<id>/`).
