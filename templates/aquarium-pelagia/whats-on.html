Feeds, talks, nights and dives

What’s on

Twelve feeds and talks every day, two more on Friday and Saturday nights, and four things you can book that take you past the glass.

The timetable

Every day

Every day at 13:00

The Open Ocean dive feed

Two aquarists go in with the food and a microphone. Thirty minutes in front of the 36-metre window, with the sharks fed by hand from a pole and the whale sharks by bucket at the surface. Be at the window by ten to one; the gallery holds four hundred and fills.
