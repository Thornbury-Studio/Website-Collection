The 36-metre window

Seven and a half million litres behind 700 mm of acrylic, twelve metres tall. Two whale sharks, a reef manta and two thousand jacks pass every few minutes. The dive feed is at 13:00, every day.

Plan a visit

Eight minutes from Marina South Pier

Out of exit B, along the waterfront and you will see the building. Allow three hours for the five halls, four if you are here for a feed. Pushchairs and wheelchairs go everywhere the visitors go; the tunnel has a lift at each end.
