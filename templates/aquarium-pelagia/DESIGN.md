# PELAGIA — design notes

**Business.** Pelagia Aquarium Pte. Ltd., a fictional open-ocean aquarium at
Marina Coastal Drive, Singapore: five halls, a 36-metre window, timed-entry
tickets (adult S$42, child S$30, senior S$32, family S$128, After Dark S$36),
annual passes, a daily timetable of twelve feeds and talks, and four bookable
experiences with dates and prices. Every number is one a visitor could act on.

**Archetype.** A floating glass dashboard over a full-bleed photograph, after
the Pinterest reference the build was briefed on: a lone silhouette against a
vast blue window, a round logo mark, centred nav, and below the hero a glass
deck of circular outlined icons, image-and-text rows and a star rating. The
reference's subject (space) is not used — an aquarium gives the same night-blue
depth and silhouette-at-glass composition with a real product behind it.

**Palette.** Abyss blue (`#050B18`) from deep water, ice-cyan (`#5EE7FF`) for
light through glass, translucent blue-white panels with `backdrop-filter`.
Every photograph is graded to the same lifted black point so the tanks and
the page read as one surface.

**Type.** Funnel Display (300–800) for display, Rethink Sans for body and UI.
Neither is used elsewhere in the collection.

**References.** The supplied glassmorphism mock; Monterey Bay Aquarium's
daily feeding schedule strip; Georgia Aquarium's timed-entry ticket flow;
teamLab Planets' dark full-bleed photography with a glass interface.

**Motion.** One reveal on entry, plus the live water on the home hero: marine
snow drifting up the tank and three shafts of surface light that lean toward
the pointer, with the photograph drifting a few pixels against it. Two
licensed clips loop muted as ambient backgrounds, playing only while on
screen. Everything else responds to a click.
