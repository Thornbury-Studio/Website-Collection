Fabric

We buy four cloths. That is the whole list.

Two of them come from the same mill in Biella and one of them we waited fourteen months for. Every piece in the range is cut from one of these four, which is why the range is six pieces and not sixty.

  1. GR‑118 Grid fleece118 g/m² · 52% recycled

    Knitted as a waffle so the pile sits in islands with channels between them. Warm air stays in the islands, sweat leaves through the channels. It is the only fleece we found that did not go slack after forty washes, and we washed nine of them forty times to find that out.

  2. AX‑96 2.5-layer laminate96 g/m² laminated · 128 g/m² bare

    Rated to 20,000 mm of water, which is more rain than Yorkshire has ever managed. We did not buy it for that. We bought it for the 20,000 g of vapour it passes in a day, because the jacket you take off is the jacket that failed. The same 40-denier yarn, woven double and left unlaminated, is the short and the cap.

  3. MN‑92 Open mesh knit92 g/m² · single jersey

    Mostly hole, held together by a knit structure fine enough to survive a race number pinned through it. It weighs almost nothing as a finished garment and you will spend the first mile checking you put it on.

  4. PW‑240 Power knit240 g/m² · four-way

    Dense enough to be opaque when you crouch to tie a lace, which is a lower bar than the industry clears. Recovers to within two per cent of its cut length after a hundred hours on the leg.

Field notes

Fourteen people, asked to be unkind.

The test group is not a focus group

Fourteen runners across Yorkshire, the Cairngorms and one very unhelpful person in Singapore. They are paid, they keep the kit, and they are explicitly asked to write down what annoyed them rather than what they liked. Six pieces have shipped. Eleven have not.

Why the seams moved

The first Dew Point had a shoulder seam where every vest has a shoulder seam, and every tester with a hydration pack came back with the same red line across the same place. It took four rounds to move it forward by eleven millimetres. That is the entire reason this company exists.

What we will not do

No seasonal colours. No limited runs. No fabric we cannot name the mill for. If a piece here is replaced it will be because it was beaten, not because it is March.

Sizing

Two fits, and we say which.

Every piece is cut either close or easy, it says so on the page, and it does not change between colours. Sizes run 1 to 6 with the chest and waist measurement printed beside each one, in centimetres, because a letter is not a measurement.
