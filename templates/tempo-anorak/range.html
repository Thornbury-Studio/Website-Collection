The range, entire

Six pieces. Four cloths. Two fits.

Weights below are the finished garment on our own scale in a size 3, not the mill’s figure for the cloth. Where a piece says how much of itself is fabric, that number is worked out from the cut area and the cloth weight — it is arithmetic, not marketing.

Shell · cut easy

Crosswind

£215

A 2.5-layer shell with a hood that stays where you put it. The brim is wired, the collar closes above the chin rather than across it, and the hem drops 40 mm at the back so it does not ride when you lean into a climb. Pit zips open one-handed while running, which took three rounds of pull design to be true.

Sizing

A letter is not a measurement.

Sizes run 1 to 6 and mean the same thing on every piece. Measure yourself, find the band, and if you sit on a line take the lower number for anything cut close and the higher one for anything cut easy.

Two fits, and we say which

Close follows the body with about 20 mm of ease at the chest: Hard Frost, Dew Point, Half Light. Easy carries 60 mm and is meant to go over something: Crosswind, Dry Spell.

It does not change between colours, it does not change between runs, and it will not quietly change next year. If a fit ever does move we will say so on this page and give the old measurements alongside the new ones.

Unsure? Write to kit@anorak.cc with your chest and waist and someone here will answer with a number.
