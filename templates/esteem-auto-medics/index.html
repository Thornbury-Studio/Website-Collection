Protected surface

Why film

The damage stops at the film.

Singapore roads are hard on paint — expressway stone chips, tight carparks, harsh UV all year round. A quality protection film takes that punishment as a sacrificial layer, and comes off clean years later with your factory paint underneath.

Stone chips & road rash

The film absorbs impacts that would chip exposed paint on bumpers and bonnet edges.

Carpark scratches

Trolley taps, bags and keys mark the film, not the panel under it.

UV & fading

A UV-stable top coat helps keep gloss and colour even across wrapped panels.

Resale-day paint

Original paint under film is one of the easiest wins when it's time to sell the car.
