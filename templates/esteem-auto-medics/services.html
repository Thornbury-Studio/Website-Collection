Paint Protection Film

Wrapped by hand,
panel by panel.

We measure your exact car, plotter-cut the film, and fit it panel by panel with wrapped edges where the panel allows — so the protection is there even where you don't look.

Two things set a PPF quote: which category your vehicle falls into and how much of it you wrap. Every coverage level is available on every category.

Sedan / Hatchback SUV / MPV Sports / Supercar
  • Coverage consultation against your parking, driving and holding plans
  • Two-stage wash and decontamination before any film touches paint
  • Plotter-cut patterns — no blades near your paint
  • Curing, edge seal and a documented quality check before handover
  • Film brand & warranty terms Being finalised

Accident repair & claims

One team, report to settlement.

Repair and claim usually mean two sets of phone calls. Here they're one job card — the people fixing the car are the same people talking to your insurer.

  1. Report

    Message us from the scene — we'll tell you exactly what photos and details to capture.

  2. Assess & file

    Damage assessed in the workshop, report and photo set prepared for your insurer.

  3. Repair

    Panel, structural and cosmetic work on Level 1, resprays upstairs on Level 4.

  4. Settle & collect

    We follow the claim through to settlement while you track the repair from your phone.
