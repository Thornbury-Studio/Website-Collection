The Night Itself

One evening a season. Gates at dusk, judging by lamplight, Best of Show called at midnight. Two hundred guests, ten entries, and no photography except the house's own — the field is dark for a reason.

Programme

Timed to the minute; held to the mood.
19:30

Gates. Guests walk in through the old customs arch; the field is still covered. Champagne, and the sound of engines cooling.

20:15

The uncovering. Ten covers come off in entry order, one lamp each. The sealed entry is uncovered last.

21:00

Judging. Three judges, white gloves, torches. Guests are asked to keep to the gravel and let the cards be written in silence.

22:30

The drive-past. Every entry runs the length of the field under its own power. This is the part people write to us about.

23:45

Best of Show, called once, from the steps. Rosettes pinned. The field stays open until the last engine is cold.
