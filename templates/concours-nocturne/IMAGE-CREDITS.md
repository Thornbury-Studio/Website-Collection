# NOCTURNE — asset credits

All photography and video is free-license material from Pexels (Pexels
License: free for commercial use, no attribution required — credited here
anyway). Downloaded 2026-08-12 at original resolution. Eleven tonally divergent
photographs were batch-graded into the house's warm-black look with the Adobe
Photoshop/Lightroom API (darks −12, contrast +10, highlights −8, warm shift
+26 tempB, saturation −6); the rest keep their genuine night light. The hero
film was graded warm in the encode (colortemperature 4600K, saturation 0.87)
and encoded with x264 `aq-mode=3` so the darkness doesn't band.
