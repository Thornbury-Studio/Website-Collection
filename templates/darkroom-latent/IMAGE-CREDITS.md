# Image credits — LATENT.

Every photograph is from Pexels under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/)
(free for commercial use, no attribution required — credited here anyway).
Each was checked for third-party marks before use. Re-encoded to WebP at
1400 px and 800 px (the negatives at 1500 and 900); nothing else altered
unless noted.

**Why the three street frames repeat:** they are the roll. The same three
photographs are the negatives in the carrier (shown inverted), the image
the bench computes a print from, and the ringed frames on the contact
sheet (shown as neighbouring frames by re-cropping, the way a real roll
has near-duplicates either side of a keeper). That repetition is the
mechanic, not thin sourcing. Every other photograph is used exactly once.
