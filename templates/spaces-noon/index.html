01 · The conviction

Sheet A-00

—

Light is the only amenity that cannot be installed.

Power can be laid, lifts can be fitted, walls can be moved. But no budget has ever bought an hour of morning for a north-facing room. When a building is wrong about light it is wrong forever — and when it is right, it is right for centuries. So that is what we buy, and that is all we advertise.

Every figure on this site — every sun-hour, bearing and altitude — is computed from the geometry of the sun over Havnsund as you read it, applied to each building's true orientation. We would rather show you arithmetic than adjectives.

03 · The survey

Method in brief · full method on the practice sheet

Orientation

Every window wall's true bearing, taken on site. The sun forgives nothing and neither does the survey: a wall eight degrees off south is recorded eight degrees off south.

Glazing

Glass area over wall area, measured pane by pane — not the brochure ratio. The Meridian Hall runs to 71 percent; most offices in this city manage 28.

Sky

What the neighbours take, the survey subtracts. Horizon obstructions are walked and measured, then the sun's real arc is run against them for every hour of the year.
