The sun never enters. That is the whole point.

Glassblowers built this roof to inspect their work by light that never moved, and the sawtooth still does exactly that: five teeth of north glass pouring an even, shadowless sky across the floor from opening to closing. Colours read true at nine in the morning and true at six at night. Nothing flares, nothing drifts, nothing needs a blind.

It is the building we show painters, retouchers, framers, ceramicists — anyone whose work is ruined by a moving highlight. The survey below will confirm what the roof promises: turn the dial and watch the sun cross the sky without once landing on the floor.
