Hollin

Hot room · 12 minutes

Heat

90 degrees on the top bench, 80 on the bottom. Water goes on the stones every ten minutes and the room lets you know about it.

Nobody talks much. You'll know when you've had enough; leave a minute before that.

  • 90 °C top bench
  • 12 min a round
  • 16 places

The rinse · 1 minute

Rinse

Out of the heat and under the shower at 38 degrees: your own temperature, give or take.

It takes the sweat off and tells your skin what's coming. Don't skip it. The plunge is shared.

  • 38 °C shower
  • 1 min

Cold plunge · 2 minutes

Cold

4 degrees. 2 tubs, 2 people each, 1.2 metres deep, so you're in to the shoulders.

First round, 1 minute. After that, 2. Breathe out for longer than you breathe in, and get out before you want to.

  • 4 °C water
  • 2 min at most
  • 1.2 m deep

Rest room · 21 °C

Then rest

You lie down. 14 loungers, blankets, no phones and no music, for 10 minutes or until your heart has come back to its own speed.

Then you go round again. 3 rounds is a session.

What happens in each room

Doors at 06:30

Outside is usually colder than the plunge

Weekdays from 06:30, weekends from 08:00. The last session starts at 21:00. Sessions start on the hour and half hour, 12 guests at a time.
