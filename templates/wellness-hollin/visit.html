What to bring

Not much

  • SwimwearWorn in every room. Shorts or a costume, nothing with metal fastenings; they get hot.
  • SandalsOr borrow a pair at the desk.
  • A water bottleWe fill it. You'll want more than you think.
  • Nothing elseTowels, robe, blanket, shampoo and a hairdryer are here.

House rules

Seven rules

There are only a few, and they're for the person next to you.

  • Rinse firstEvery time, before every plunge.
  • Phones stay in lockersPast the lockers there are no cameras of any kind.
  • Low voicesIn the hot room and the rest room, quiet. Talk in the changing room.
  • Sit on a towelOn every bench, every tier.
  • No glassBring a bottle that won't break on tile.
  • Over 18Younger guests only in the private room, with an adult.
  • Leave when you need toNo round has to be finished. Tell whoever is on the floor if you feel faint.
