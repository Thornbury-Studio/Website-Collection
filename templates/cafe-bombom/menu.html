Ordering

How it works.

How big is a bingsu?

Big enough for two. Most tables order one between them with a drink each — that is the usual way to do it.

What should I get first?

Chocolate if you want the one people come for. K-Ogok if you would rather something toasted and nutty than sweet. Dalgona if you like burnt sugar.

What is a bomcaron?

The house macaron — thicker and less sweet than the French kind, with a heavier filling. Flavours rotate.

Is there anything besides dessert?

Yes — more than fifty drinks including hot and iced coffee, lattes, frappes, sodas and yogurt smoothies, plus croffles and cheesecake.

Do you take orders ahead?

Order at the counter on Level 4. Everything is made when you ask for it.
