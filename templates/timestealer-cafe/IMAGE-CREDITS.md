## Grading

All seven frames went through one identical pass (`scratchpad/grade.py`): a warm
channel shift toward the parchment ground, a 28 % smoothstep S-curve, colour to
0.92, brightness to 1.02. No per-image tweaking — the point was to make seven
photographers look like one kitchen.

## Rejected

Frames that were licensed-adjacent candidates but broke the set, kept here so the
same ones are not re-proposed:

- **209445609** (homemade chocolate muffins, vintage background) — cold grey
  concrete ground; fought the warm palette even after the grade.
- **552054138** (grilled chicken teriyaki, black background) — pure black
  backdrop, too dramatic for a heartland café.
- **427462208 / 675873275** (women baking at home) — identifiable faces.
  Deliberately excluded: a stock model must not stand in for a real, named
  person ("Mama Moon") on a site pitched to that person's business. Story-page
  imagery is hands-only for this reason.

## On replacement

Every one of these is a stand-in for photography the café already has. On
engagement, the whole set should be replaced with real shots of the actual unit,
the actual dishes and the actual kitchen — at which point this file becomes a
record of what the pitch used, not what the site ships.
