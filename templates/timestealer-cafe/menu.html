The counter menu

What’s on today.

Everything except the bakes is cooked when you order it, so give it a few minutes. Anything marked Daily changes with what the kitchen has in — ask at the counter and someone will tell you what came out this morning.

Ordering ahead

Call the counter on 8670 0039 if you want a whole cake, or a few bowls at once for the office. Give it a day’s notice for anything large.

Paying

Cash, NETS and CDC vouchers are all fine at the counter.
