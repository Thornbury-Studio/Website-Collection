Before you come

Questions we get asked.

Can I sit down?

It is a small unit, so seating is limited and most orders are taken away. If you want to eat in, come outside the lunch rush and you will have a better chance.

Do you take CDC vouchers?

Yes. Cash, NETS and CDC vouchers are all accepted at the counter.

How long does an order take?

Rice bowls and croissants are cooked when you order, so give it a few minutes at the counter. Bakes are ready to go.

Can I order a whole cake?

Yes — call 8670 0039. Give a day’s notice where you can, especially if you need it for a particular date.

Is the food halal-certified?

No, the café is not halal-certified.

What is on the menu today?

The fixed items are on the menu page. Anything marked Daily — the bowl of the day, the bakes, the potato toppings — changes with what the kitchen has in, so call or ask at the counter.
