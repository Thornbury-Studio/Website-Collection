# AMICUS — media provenance

Every image on this page was generated for this template on **6 August 2026**.
Nothing is licensed stock, no photograph of a real person is used, and no third-party
copyrighted artwork is involved.

## The firm

AMICUS is a **fictional law firm**. The attorneys pictured are not real people — each
portrait is an AI-generated depiction of an invented character, and the page's footer
says so in plain language. Every case result, statistic, testimonial, and biography is
written fiction, chosen to be internally consistent, not drawn from any real matter.

The brand mark (the "A" in a rounded plate) is hand-drawn SVG in `index.html`,
original to this template.

## Generation

**Tool:** Higgsfield MCP
**Image model:** `nano_banana_flash` (Google), 1K, text-to-image

All four portraits share one prompt scaffold — soft window light from the left, warm
ivory backdrop, muted earth-tone tailoring — so they read as one photo shoot. Prompts
explicitly asked for **fictional people** and **no text, no lettering, no logos**
anywhere in frame.

## Post-processing

PNG renders were cover-cropped and resized locally with Pillow, then encoded to WebP
(quality 80, `method=6`) — portraits at 600 × 800, the office at 1280 × 714.
Five images total **241 KB**.

## Figures

Every number on the page — dollars recovered, matters resolved, success rates, callback
windows — is **invented** and exists only to demonstrate the layout. The footer carries
the disclaimer; nothing on the page is legal advice.
