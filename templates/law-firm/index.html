The situation. A rear-end collision at a loading dock left our client unable to work. The insurer’s first offer — made while he was still in a brace — was $90,000.

What we did. We declined, politely. Then we built the file they should have feared: vocational experts, a life-care plan, and the dock’s own maintenance logs.

Where it landed. $2.3 million, settled eleven days before trial. He never set foot in a courtroom.

“They talked to me like a person, and to the insurer like a wall.”

What we did. An emergency motion within 48 hours, then something rarer: a parenting plan so specific and so fair the judge adopted it nearly verbatim.

Where it landed. Primary custody, school stability, and holidays that don’t need a lawyer to schedule anymore.

What we did. We requested the restructure paperwork. There wasn’t any. Discovery surfaced messages that made the real reason plain.

Where it landed. $640,000 and a neutral reference — plus the safety issue got fixed.

What we did. A forensic document examiner, a quiet deposition, and — just as important — mediation that kept three siblings speaking to each other.
