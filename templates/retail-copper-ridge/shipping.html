Policy

Shipping & restrictions

Copper Ridge ships ammunition, primers, and powder by ground hazmat from Missoula, Montana. We sell to adults 21 and older. Card payment is authorized at fulfillment.

Ground hazmat

Loaded ammunition and primers are classified as ORM-D / limited quantity for ground carriers. We do not offer air, overnight, or PO Box delivery on hazmat lines. Expect 5–8 business days to most Lower 48 addresses after the order leaves the floor.

Signature & ID

Every ammunition order requires an adult signature. The carrier may ask for government ID that matches the ship-to name. We do not leave ammo on doorsteps.

Destinations we do not serve

We cannot ship ammunition, primers, or powder to:

  • AK
  • HI

Individual SKUs (especially defensive hollow points and certain rifle loads) list additional blocked states on the product page. Checkout re-checks those rules against your selected state.

Returns

Unopened factory-sealed ammunition may be returned within 14 days for store credit. We do not accept opened boxes, mixed lots, or powder canisters once shipped. Reloading presses and die sets follow the same sealed-carton rule.
