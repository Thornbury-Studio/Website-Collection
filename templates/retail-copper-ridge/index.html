The warehouse

Built around the pallet, not the slogan.

Copper Ridge pulls orders from a single floor in Missoula. We stock the calibers people actually shoot — 9mm cases, .223 bricks, AA targets, Mini-Mags — and a short reloading aisle for primers, powder, and dies.

No firearms. No FFL transfers. Just cartridges, components, and honest ship rules.

How we ship

Ground only. Signature on the door.

Ammunition, primers, and powder travel hazmat ground. We do not ship to Alaska or Hawaii. Some defensive and green-tip SKUs are blocked in additional states — those flags appear on the product page and again at checkout.
