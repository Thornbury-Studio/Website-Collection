# STRAKE — product truth

This file plus this project's own `DESIGN.md` (see
`C:\School\Personal\Company\DESIGN-SYSTEM\DESIGN.md` for the shared taste
system both build on) is what `frontend-design`, `impeccable`, and any
design-critique skill read before touching a pixel.

## Who this is for
Someone who already owns the obvious cars and is pricing a £250,000
commission from a small yard: they want to see how it is built, who builds
it, and what a hull number costs with their options on it. Second reader: a
motoring journalist looking for the story and a press contact.

## What it does / sells
One car, the Carvel — a front-mid-engined, naturally aspirated V8 grand
tourer with a hand-formed aluminium body — forty a year, sold by deposit in
order of receipt. The site shows the car, explains the boat-yard method, tells
the yard's hundred-year story, publishes the three current press releases,
lets the buyer specify paint, wheels and seats against a real render and a
real price, and takes an enquiry.

## Voice & tone
Foreman's plain speech. Short sentences. Real numbers. No adjectives a buyer
could not check in the shed.

## Constraints that are actually load-bearing right now
1. The configurator must visibly change the car for every option that claims
   to — no swatch that does nothing.
2. White ground, charcoal, one bronze. No second metallic, no dark mode.

## What makes this one memorable
A car sold as a hull: the bronze rubbing strake along the sheer line, the
cedar bucks from 1934, and a timeline that spends fifty-eight years on boats
before the first car appears.
