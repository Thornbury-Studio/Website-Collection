# LUME — design notes

ClearPath (Framer / Anton Drukarov) structure adapted for a skincare studio:
soft full-bleed hero, ritual grid, philosophy, client stories, how-it-works,
pricing, journal, FAQ, booking form.

## Brand

- Mark: `lume—skin` (em-dash lockup, ClearPath cadence)
- Voice: calm, clinical-gentle, never “miracle”
- Offer: facial rituals + at-home protocols, not a marketplace dump

## Visual

- Cool mist / celadon / soft charcoal — not warm cream + terracotta
- Display: Cormorant Garamond; UI: Outfit
- Hero is edge-to-edge video (Mixkit) with soft sage wash
- Pill CTAs, hairline rules, generous negative space

## Motion

1. Hero video slow Ken-Burns scale (CSS)
2. Scroll reveals on section blocks
3. Stat counters + FAQ accordion
