Saturdays at the door.

The Tai Seng unit rolls up its door every Saturday from 9 to 1. Come and taste all five, buy a bottle for S$5 or six for S$27, bring your empties back. It is five minutes from Tai Seng MRT, but the estate is all identical blocks, so message us and we send the unit number and a photo of the door.

When
Saturdays, 9 am – 1 pm. Closed on public holidays and one Saturday a month, which we post on Instagram the week before.
Where
Tai Seng industrial estate, five minutes on foot from the MRT. Unit number by message.
Pay
PayNow or cash. No card machine, sorry.
Bring
Empties, for 50 cents off each new bottle.
