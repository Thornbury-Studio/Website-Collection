Your first visit

No surprises. We promise.

Here’s the whole thing, start to finish — about fifty minutes, and the only paperwork is one page.

  1. 1

    Say hello, get comfy

    Tea, coffee, or the good hot chocolate. One page of paperwork — we fill the rest from your old dentist so you don’t have to remember dates.

  2. 2

    A look around, on the big screen

    Photos and a gentle check-up, shown to you as we go. You’ll see exactly what we see — most mouths need less work than their owners fear.

  3. 3

    A plan, with prices, on paper

    What’s urgent, what can wait, what it costs — written down, insurance checked before you leave. You decide at home, not in the chair.

Does it hurt?

Our whole practice is built around the answer being no. Numbing done slowly, checked before we start, and you can stop us with a raised hand at any moment — that rule is real and everyone here follows it.

What does a check-up cost without insurance?

$95 flat — exam, cleaning, and photos included. If you join the in-house plan it’s covered twice a year. Prices live on paper, not in a phone call maze.

I haven’t been in years. Will I get judged?

No. Half our new patients open with that sentence. Teeth are teeth; we fix them forward from today, and nobody here does lectures.

Can you see my whole family in one visit?

Yes — that’s most of our Saturdays. Book a family block and we’ll run two chairs side by side; small kids can sit in with you.

What about emergencies?

Call before 2pm and we’ll see you the same day, patched and out of pain — even if you’ve never been here before. The follow-up plan comes after the relief does.
