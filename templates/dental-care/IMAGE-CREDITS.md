# BRIGHTSIDE — media provenance

This template contains **no photography and no AI-generated imagery**. Every visual —
the arched-window hero scene, the happy tooth mark, the smile arc, the service icons,
the comfort-menu icons, and the three team portraits — is **hand-written inline SVG**,
original to this template.

## Why there are no photos

The template was built after the Higgsfield generation budget ran out, so imagery had
to come from genuinely copyright-free sources. A search of Openverse (CC0 / public
domain marks only) and Wikimedia Commons on **7 August 2026** found nothing usable:

- Warm, editorial dental photography effectively does not exist under CC0/PDM. What
  exists is museum art (a 1937 dental-chair painting), decades-old clinical snapshots,
  and equipment photos under fluorescent light.
- The public-domain photos that do show people are U.S. Navy clinic photographs of
  identifiable service members. License aside, presenting real, identifiable people as
  a fictional practice's staff or patients would misrepresent them — so real-face
  photos were ruled out for the team section on principle, the same reason the AMICUS
  and AUREL templates used generated or illustrated people.
- Several Wikimedia results were promotional uploads from real dental practices;
  reusing another clinic's marketing photos in a template would be wrong even where
  the license technically allows it.

The illustration system is the honest fallback, and the site is designed so real
photography can slot in later: the hero art, and the three `member-art` blocks are
self-contained and swappable. The hub card for this template is marked
**"In progress"** until that happens; the team section also says on-page that the
portraits are illustrated.

## Figures

Every figure — years in practice, patient counts, prices, hours, reviews, and the
dentists themselves — is **invented**, chosen to be internally consistent. The footer
carries the disclaimer; nothing on the page is medical advice.
