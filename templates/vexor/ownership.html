FAQ

Do I need a Class 2 licence for every machine?

LINE, TRACE and VOLT can be demonstrated on Class 2A where power caps allow; ARC requires Class 2. We confirm licence class when you book.

Can I compare finance with my bank’s rate?

Yes. The on-site calculator is a planning estimate. Change the interest field to match your lender and re-read monthly and total payable.

Is trade-in available?

Both centres assess clean Singapore-registered machines during a ride booking. Bring log card and service history.

How do I add machines to Compare?

Open any model page and tap Add to compare. The tray holds three bikes and persists in this browser until you clear it.
