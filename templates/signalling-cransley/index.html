    Schedule of works

    What comes through the door

    1. 01

      Frame overhaul

      Strip to the quadrant plate. Catch rods, catch blocks, handles, bearings. We re-bush rather than re-line where the casting allows it, and we return the frame with the same maker's plate it arrived with.

      6–16 weeks
    2. 02

      Tappet locking

      New tappets cut to a locking table we draw with you and test on the bench before it goes near a box. If the table is wrong the frame will be wrong, quietly, for thirty years. We prove every lock both ways.

      Bench-proved
    3. 03

      Semaphore arms & spectacles

      Arm blades in steel or fibre to your railway's pattern, spectacle plates re-glazed with proper signal glass, lamp cases re-tinned. Paint to a matched sample, not to a chart.

      Arms from stock
    4. 04

      Block instruments

      Needle instruments, plungers, bells and repeaters. Coils rewound, contacts dressed, cases re-polished. We return them calibrated against the shop standard and bell-tested on the circuit you will actually run.

      By arrangement
    5. 05

      Gate & barrier gear

      Wheel-worked gates, stay bars, wicket locks, gate stops. Barrier machines where a railway has gone that way. The gate is the part the public touches, so it is the part we test hardest.

      Site work
    6. 06

      Survey & testing

      A day in the box, a locking table on paper, a written report on what is worn, what is wrong and what will fail first. Many railways stop here, fix two things themselves, and call us back in a decade.

      One day, fixed fee
