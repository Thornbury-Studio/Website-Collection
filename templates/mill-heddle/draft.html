The drafting room

Draw the cloth before we weave it.

This is the tool the mill plans a cloth on, with the yarn we actually stock. Press a cell and the cloth underneath changes. When it is right, send it: we sett it for our lambswool, weave thirty centimetres, post the sample and quote for the length.

1 · Top

Threading

One column per warp end, one row per shaft. The filled square says which shaft carries that end. Every end is on exactly one shaft.

2 · Top right

Tie-up

One column per treadle. The filled squares are the shafts that treadle lifts when it is pressed.

3 · Right

Treadling

One row per pick, top to bottom. The filled square is the treadle pressed for that pick; press two for a combined lift.

4 · Below

The cloth

Where a lifted shaft carries an end, you see warp. Everywhere else, weft. The colour strips along the edges are the yarn order.

Forty ends across. Drag the draft sideways for the tie-up and the treadling.
