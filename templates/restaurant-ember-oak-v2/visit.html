Getting here

        

          
By train. Hudson is two hours from Penn Station on the Empire Service. From the platform it is ten minutes on foot, uphill all the way. Check the time of the last train south before you order the ribeye.

          
By car. There is metered parking on Tanner Street, free after six, and a public lot one block north. We have no lot of our own.

          
Finding the door. Brick front, green door, no sign bigger than the brass plate. In the evening you will smell it before you see it.

        

      


      

        
Asked often

        

          

            Do I need to book?
            
Friday and Saturday, yes, usually a week ahead. Midweek you can often walk in. The eight counter seats are never booked, and if you arrive at five there is a good chance of one.

          

          

            How long do we have the table?
            
Two hours for two people, two and a half for larger tables. We hold a booking for fifteen minutes, so ring if you are running late and we will keep it.

          

          

            Is there much for vegetarians?
            
Four starters, the cauliflower, three of the sides and all the sweet things. Vegans have fewer choices but will not go hungry. The menu page marks every dish.

          

          

            Can you cook for an allergy?
            
Often, but not always. One grill and one oven do everything, so we cannot keep fish, flour or nuts fully apart. Tell us when you book and we will say plainly what is and is not safe.

          

          

            Do you take a deposit or charge for no-shows?
            
No. We do not take card details to hold a table. Please just tell us if you are not coming, because the table goes to someone standing at the door.

          

          

            Can we have the whole room?
            
On a Monday or Tuesday, when we are otherwise closed, for up to forty people. We cook one menu for everyone from the fire. Call to talk it through.

          

          

            Can I bring a dog?
            
Not inside, apart from service animals. In warm months there are two tables on the sidewalk where dogs are welcome.

          

        

      


      

        
Privacy

        

          
This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. The table form above works entirely in your browser: what you type is turned into an email in your own mail app, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else until you press send there.

          
When you do email or call, we keep your name, number and booking details in the reservations book for ninety days and then delete them. We do not share them or add you to a mailing list. To have your details removed sooner, write to tables@emberandoak.example.
