# Ember & Oak (v2): image and font credits

All photographs are from Unsplash under the Unsplash License (free for
commercial use, no attribution required). None are Unsplash+ items. Each was
downloaded at 2400px on 2026-10-02, cropped where noted, and exported to WebP
at 800 and 1600 wide. No colour grading, compositing or generative editing.
No AI-generated imagery is used anywhere in the template.

Photo page: `https://unsplash.com/photos/<ID>`

The menu was written to the photographs, not the other way round. Dishes
without a matching licensed photo (leeks, beets, merguez, cauliflower,
chicken, pork chop, ribeye, the sides, apples, chocolate pot) are text only.

Two judgement calls worth knowing about: the beef photo is described on the
site as sirloin cap, which is what the cut with its fat cap looks like, but
the source does not name the cut. The fish photo is described as trout; the
source describes it only as grilled fish.
