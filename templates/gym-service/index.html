The FORGE protocol

Training is not
a collection of workouts.

It is a system. Four standards govern every block, every coaching decision, and every result we measure.

FORGE / METHOD ATX — 30.2672° N
Consistency target 48 WEEKS
System active 01 / 04
Standard 01 · Consistency

Discipline
over motivation.

Motivation is weather. Your training rhythm is infrastructure. We build the week around your real life, then repeat the work long enough for it to matter.

Show upDo the workStack the weeks
Standard 02 · Coaching

Coached.
Never supervised.

A coach sees what an app cannot: the rep that changed, the load that is too light, and the day you need restraint more than intensity.

WatchAdjustGive feedback
Standard 03 · Progression

Proof,
not guesswork.

Every block opens with a baseline and closes with evidence. Strength, engine, and movement quality are recorded so the next decision is earned.

BaselineBuildTest again
Standard 04 · Longevity

Recovery
is training.

Adaptation happens after the hard work. Mobility, sleep habits, and planned deloads protect the next session—and the next decade.
