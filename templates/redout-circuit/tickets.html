Round 06 · Lisbon · Cement Hall · 03 October

Tickets

One night. Doors at six, qualifying at seven, the final under the lights at ten. Three ways in, and the pilot’s box puts any pilot’s goggle feed on your own headset for the whole final.

Capacity 4,200Doors 18:00Final 22:00Countdown 03 Oct

The venue

Cement Hall,
Lisbon

A hundred and ten metres of empty kiln hall on the river at Alcântara, with the columns still in. Twelve gates, two tunnels through the old hopper bays and a split gate on the long straight the width of a doorway. The rail runs the full length of it.

The course for Round 06. Gate 01 is the start gate; the red mark is the timing line.
