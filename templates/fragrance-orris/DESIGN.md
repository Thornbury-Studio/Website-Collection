# DESIGN.md — ORRIS

## World

**Product house that beats generic retail.** Clearer than a WooCommerce grid (Bottle Bottle baseline): real product photography first, readable shop tiles with stock/price, then a live compound bench no catalogue site has.

## Surface

- Paper `#f6f5f2` / white panels
- Ink `#111`, mute `#6a6a66`, signal `#c45c26`
- Type: IBM Plex Mono (mark, specs) + IBM Plex Sans (body)
- Product tiles use formula-colour stages + bottle silhouette — denser than flat name/price cards

## First viewport

Full-bleed product still, bottle readable. Brand `orris`. One line. Shop + compound CTAs. No costume ghost type, no dark crush overlay.

## Signature

Accord builder remains the killer interaction after the shop strip.

## Refuse

Dark empty wireframes, cream-terracotta AI luxury, purple glow, sale-badge clutter, script lifestyle overlays.
