How the archive works

01 Trawl

Haul the net and five plates surface, face down. Every trawl is free. Some lights are rarer than others; the deep does not negotiate.

02 Log

Turned plates join your Log — the Survey's binder of record. Empty slots stay embossed on the page, so you always know what's still down there.

03 Complete

Finish spreads, earn the Survey's stamps, and close out Descent I. The archive remembers everything on this device, and nothing anywhere else.
