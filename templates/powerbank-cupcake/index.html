An explanation, since you asked

Why “Cupcake”?

Our engineers spent eleven months on cell chemistry, thermal design, and firmware that treats your battery kindly. They spent eleven minutes on the name. A cupcake is small, dense, reliably improves your day, and nobody has ever been intimidated by one.

We stand by both decisions. The chemistry got us 500 charge cycles at 80% health. The name gets a small smile at airport security, every single time. Engineering is about trade-offs; this wasn’t one.

Can it charge a laptop?

Slowly, honestly. 30W keeps a light laptop alive through a long meeting; it won’t feed a gaming rig. We’d rather tell you that here than let a product page imply otherwise.

Why is there no app?

Because it’s a battery. The number on the front is the app. Firmware updates ship over the cable on the rare day we need one.

Is the name a joke?

The name is a joke. The 500-cycle cell, the aluminium shell, the two-year warranty, and the thermal firmware are not. We find this division of labor works well.
