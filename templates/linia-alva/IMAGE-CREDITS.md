## Editing applied

Video: trims to best passages; unified winter grade (S-curve + cool slate
shadows via ffmpeg curves/colorbalance/eq; the shoulder-season Wiesen clip
frost-graded); some clips crossfade-baked into seamlessless loops; one clip
carries a 1.05 punch-in. Encoded x264 (aq-mode=3) at 1440/1080 + mobile tiers,
faststart, no audio tracks. Stills: unified cool grade (Pillow), WebP tiers.
Audio: loop-baked with acrossfade, loudnorm.

## Brand note

LINIA ALVA, its stations, timetable, fares and route are fictional
(portfolio work). The footage shows real winter railways of Graubünden;
trims and crops minimise legible real-operator marks; where stock passes
close to camera, incidental markings may remain visible, as in any
editorial footage.
