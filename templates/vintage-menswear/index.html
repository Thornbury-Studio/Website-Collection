The Shop

Morrow & Finch has stood at 14 Ledbury Row, London since 1934 — first as a tailor's, then as an outfitter's, always under the same name over the door. Everything in this catalogue is cut, sewn or knitted in Britain.

Orders are taken through the order book; collection from the shop is free and the post is four shillings and sixpence — which is to say £4.50, we have modernised some things. Alterations are included in the price of any tailoring.
