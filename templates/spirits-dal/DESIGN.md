# DAL — design notes

Bellussi shop (bellussi.com/shop) system adapted for Korean spirits:
age gate, bottle-over-type hero, black/white section flip, line cards,
shop grid, mixology / world CTAs.

## Brand

- **DAL** (달) — moon · Korean soju, makgeolli, yakju, limited reserve
- Tagline: Under the moon
- Lines: Classic Soju · Craft Makgeolli · Aged Yakju · Moon Reserve

## Visual

- Black `#000` · white `#FFF` · accent red `#E10600` (Bellussi cue)
- Logo serif: Cormorant Garamond · UI: Mulish (Muli successor)
- Spotlight bottle photography on black; centered white editorial blocks

## Effects

1. Age gate with bottle hero
2. Full-screen nav overlay
3. Bottle overlapping oversized type
4. Scroll reveals
5. Mini cart drawer (localStorage demo)
6. Back-to-top
