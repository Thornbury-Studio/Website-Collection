# THE BRASS OX — image provenance

THE BRASS OX is a **fictional restaurant**. The name, the ox mark, the address, the menu and
every line of copy are original to this template. No real restaurant's identity, dishes or
prices are used.

## Photography — licensed Adobe Stock (free tier)

All 31 photographs were searched, licensed and downloaded on **7 August 2026** through the
Adobe Stock connector, free-tier assets only (`pricing: "free"`, license state
`just_purchased`). Nothing is scraped, and nothing here is AI-generated.

The set spans many photographers, so every frame runs through one identical local grade
(Pillow: +6% saturation, +7% contrast, +1% brightness, warm channel shift) — deliberately
richer and warmer than the KIYO template's near-neutral pass, because char and caramelisation
are the point of a grill house. Dishes are cropped **4:3 landscape** at 800 px for the
photo-top cards; the hero is 1920 × 1080.

## What is real and what is not

The ordering flow is a working demonstration. The ticket is genuine (localStorage), the
modifier arithmetic is exact to the cent — including negative modifiers such as the half rack
of ribs — validation is real, and the submit goes nowhere. The Apple Pay and Google Pay
buttons are visual stand-ins that never open a payment sheet; the card fields check format
only; the delivery-zone check is a regex against Chicago 606xx ZIPs, not a real geocoder.
The confirmation screen says all of this out loud.
