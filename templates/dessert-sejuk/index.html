02° · House law

Ice waits for no one.

There is no delivery, and there never will be. Snow shaved at noon is water by half past. So we do it the only honest way: your chit starts the blade, and your ice is born about ninety seconds before you eat it.

04° · The house

One block, two days.

Fast-frozen ice is cloudy, brittle and loud under the blade. Ours freezes for forty-eight hours in small tanks, so it comes off the machine as something closer to snow — dense, clear, and quiet. Then the syrup kitchen gets involved.
