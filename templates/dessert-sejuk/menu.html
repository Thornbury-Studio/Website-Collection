The board

Eight peaks, a warm counter, and things in bottles.

Tap an ice to size it — solo, or berdua for two spoons — and stack the top. Your chit starts the blade; you pay at the counter. Every ice can be shaved onto coconut snow instead of milk, just say so in the note.
