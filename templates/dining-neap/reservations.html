Reservations

The counter holds fourteen.

Two seatings a night, at 17:45 and 20:45. Ask for a night; the counter will tell you which menu the moon has planned for it.

Open now — nights through —.

Next release — —, when — opens.

  • 01

    Parties of four at most. The counter is a row, not a room; larger tables would have to shout.

  • 02

    The menu is taken whole. Allergies in writing 72 hours ahead, and the kitchen will meet them properly rather than by subtraction.

  • 03

    A deposit of 120 per guest holds the seat and comes off the bill. Inside 72 hours it stays with the boats, who have already been paid.

  • 04

    Children are welcome at the early seating from twelve years — the age at which a nine-course argument about adjectives becomes interesting.

  • 05

    The telephone rings in an empty room during service. Write instead; writing gets answered between tides.

  • 06

    An evening at the counter can be given to someone else — ask, and a card in ink will be posted to them.
