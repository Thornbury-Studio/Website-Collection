Good to know

Before you come.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk in any time we’re open and we’ll fit you in if there’s a chair. Evenings and weekends get busy, so a quick call ahead saves you waiting.

How long does a set take?

An express manicure is about twenty minutes. A full gel set with art can take up to two hours. Tell us what you want when you call and we’ll give you a realistic time.

Can you remove gel from another salon?

Yes. We soak it off gently rather than filing it away. Removal is free when you’re having a new set done the same visit.

I’ve got a picture of what I want. Is that okay?

Please bring it — a screenshot is the clearest way to show us. We’ll tell you honestly whether it will work on your nail length and what it will cost.

Do you do men’s nails?

Yes, manicures and pedicures both. Shape, cuticle tidy and buff, with colour only if you want it.
