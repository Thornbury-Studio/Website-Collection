# THORNBURY DIGITAL v4 — direction

Paper showroom. The work is the picture.

Apple films the product, then puts a header on it. We do the same: one
cinema plate with a client header invented on top, then the rest of the
collection as stills — not the same five sites on a loop.

## Two materials

1. **Paper** — the chrome. `#f7f4ef` / ink / madder. Set as the inner-page default.
2. **Sites** — every picture is either a live collection template, or a
   cinema plate dressed as one. One clip, one job. Never reused.

Internet footage is allowed as the *body* of an invented site. Higgsfield
remakes a locked frame later. Credits stay in `IMAGE-CREDITS.md`.
