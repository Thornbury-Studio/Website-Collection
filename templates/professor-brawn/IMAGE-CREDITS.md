# Image credits — Professor Brawn Café redesign

the organisation's own website, profbrawn.com.sg, on 16 Aug 2026, then
re-encoded to WebP at delivery sizes. No stock photography and no generated
imagery is used anywhere in this template. Copyright in all photography and
brand artwork remains with Professor Brawn / Autism Resource Centre
(Singapore); assets are used here in a redesign of that organisation's own
website.

Notes

- Interiors identified as the closed Enabling Village bistro (`ev-*`) are used
  only in historical/story contexts, never presented as a current outlet.
- The live site publishes no photograph of the Tampines café; its cards use a
  crest treatment rather than a mislabelled photo. (The live site currently
  reuses an EV-era photo for Tampines with an outdated alt text.)
- Menu data in `js/menu-data.js` was transcribed from the official menu PDFs
  at /documents/menu/ (dine-in C1 + TP, takeaway, and the Feb 2024 venue
  package set); recheck against those PDFs before any production use.
- Rejected from the set: slideshow slides 01–03 and both `venue-ev-outlet.jpg`
  and `pb-outlet-ri.jpg` (baked-in text overlays, or they depict closed
  outlets in a current-looking context).
