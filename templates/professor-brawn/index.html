Meet the Professor

Created by a child.
Run by a community.

Professor Brawn is a superhero who is smart, kind and strong — drawn by a child on the autism spectrum. In 2009, a group of parents and friends borrowed his cape and opened a café built on a simple belief: everyone deserves the chance to contribute and shine.

Today the cafés are run by Autism Resource Centre (Singapore) as inclusive worksites, where team members of different abilities train, work and shine — supported by job coaches, valued by colleagues, and fuelled by your lunch order.
