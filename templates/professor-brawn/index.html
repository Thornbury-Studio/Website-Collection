Meet the team

Trained here.
Hired for real.

Common Ground started as a single stall run by two job coaches who wanted their trainees somewhere real to prove themselves — not a classroom, an actual kitchen with actual customers. In 2013 that stall became a café.

Today both kitchens are run day-to-day by a team trained through Bridgework Singapore, a Singapore employment-support programme — paid properly, rostered like any other job, and backed by a job coach whenever they need one.
