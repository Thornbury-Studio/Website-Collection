Reservations are answered by a person, usually the same day, never on Sundays. You pay when we confirm, not before.

Not yet. Tallow-based perfume sits awkwardly in most customs categories and we would rather not guess.

Returns · 30 days, sealed

Unopened bottles back within thirty days, full refund. Opened ones we cannot resell, so we will not pretend otherwise.

Storage

Somewhere cool and dark. Tallow keeps the scent stable for about three years; after that it softens rather than turns.
