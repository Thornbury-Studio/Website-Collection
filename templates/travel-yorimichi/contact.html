Before you ask

Questions we get

Is the flight really in the price?

Yes. Every published price includes a return economy ticket from Singapore on a scheduled airline, booked as a group. If you would rather use miles or fly a different route, we take S$780 off the price and you meet the group at the first hotel.

How much walking is there?

City days are ten to fourteen kilometres on foot at a slow pace, with train legs between. Every tour page lists its pace. If you use a stick or need a rest day, tell us; the free afternoons are built for exactly that.

Can I travel alone?

About a third of our travellers do. Twin-share pairs you with another solo traveller of the same gender, or take a single room at the listed supplement. Groups of twelve are small enough that nobody eats alone.

What if a departure does not fill?

Every departure runs with four or more travellers, and we confirm 60 days out. We have cancelled two departures in twelve years; both groups moved dates or were refunded in full, including the deposit.

Do I need a visa?

Singapore passport holders do not, for stays up to 90 days. Other passports: tell us when you enquire and we will check the current rules with you before you pay a deposit.
