A neighbourhood salon on Tampines Street 91 that has traded since 2000 with no website at all — just scattered directory entries and a 232-follower Facebook page. One fast page gives them a digital home: services, hours, directions and a live open/closed pill, with call and WhatsApp always one thumb away. Built the efficient way — six licensed stock photographs unified by a single normalising grade instead of generated imagery, one self-hosted 26 KB variable font, and a 92 KB mobile critical path.

ESTEEM AUTO MEDICS

A PPF-first rebuild proposal for the Sin Ming workshop, anchored to their own black-and-gold business card rather than a template look. One drawn-once coverage map carries the whole site: it explains the coverage levels in the hero, sits beside the three vehicle-category packages, and lights up per-panel as the live job map in the customer tracker and staff console — both running on one shared demo store, so advancing a stage in the console updates the owner’s tracking page in real time. A second interaction repaints a curved body panel under booth lighting — spray sweeping across it, clearcoat and flake shifting with the finish — to show the Level 4 division does colour changes. Pricing, film brand and warranty ship as designed “being finalised” states pending the client’s confirmation; nothing is invented.
