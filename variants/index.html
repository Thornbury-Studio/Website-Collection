  3. Variant 03 PHOSPHOR One gun, one colour. Everything is type and hairlines, and each screenshot is dithered live on canvas into amber phosphor dots. Function-key bar for navigation. VT323 only · single amber phosphor on near-black
  4. Variant 04 VECTOR Drawn with a beam. Six lanes down a glowing wireframe well you rotate through; no fills, no pixel grid, every path stroked three times for real bloom. Michroma · Martian Mono · cyan strokes on pure black
  5. Variant 05 CHIRASHI The arcade's paper, not its screen. Three fluorescent spot inks on black stock, halftoned images, headlines printed twice and a hair out of register. Dela Gothic One · Zen Kaku Gothic · fluoro pink & cyan
