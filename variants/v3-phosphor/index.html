> OPERATOR NOTE

Every site here starts from a physical or graphic world that belongs to its subject — a wood fire, a loading bay, a lit cabinet — and the layout is derived from that world rather than from a component library.

The engineering rule underneath is simple: bake once, animate cheap. Noise, geometry and layout are precomputed at initialisation; per frame there is a lerp and maybe one pair of trig calls. That is why a 2.5 MB Three.js scene still holds its frame rate on a laptop.

No build step. No framework. No bundler. Open any folder in the repository and the site runs from the file system.
